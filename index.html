Our Story

We teach Spanish the way it's lived.

Born in the mountains of Colombia, built for curious minds around the world.

Fundador

Carlos Herrera

Founder & Lead Instructor · Medellín, Colombia 🇨🇴

🎓 MA Linguistics, Univ. de Antioquia 📜 DELE Certified Examiner ⭐ 10+ Years Teaching
"I didn't just want to teach grammar rules. I wanted to share the music, the warmth, the joy of speaking Spanish the way we Colombians do — with heart."

Carlos founded Español con Sabor in 2016 after years of teaching in international schools across Bogotá and Medellín. Frustrated by textbook-heavy approaches that left students sounding robotic, he developed a cultural immersion methodology that weaves Colombian music, literature, food, and daily life into every lesson.

Today, the school has grown to a team of 8 certified teachers, 500+ students from 12 countries, and a mission: make Colombian Spanish accessible, joyful, and genuinely transformative.
